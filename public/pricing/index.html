What sets the price

What affects the price of your bookkeeping?

Four things decide how much work your books take each month. We look at all 4 in the consultation before we quote.

Number of accountsEach bank and credit card account is reconciled every month.

Transaction volumeA business with a handful of transactions a month needs less time than one with hundreds.

Condition of the booksBooks that are current cost less to keep up than books that need repair first.

Level of servicePayables, receivables, custom reports and a month-end close add to the scope.

Do you lock clients into long-term contracts?

No. Monthly bookkeeping at Duvell Bookkeeping Services has no long-term contract. Your proposal explains how the service runs and how to stop it.

Pricing questions

What else do people ask about cost?

Why is there no price list on the website?

Two businesses of the same size can need very different amounts of work. A free consultation lets us see your accounts and your books first, so the flat price in your proposal fits the real job.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The first consultation is a free 30-minute call by phone or video, with no pressure and no obligation to sign up.

Do you charge by the hour?

Proposals from Duvell Bookkeeping Services quote a flat price for the agreed scope, so you know the cost before work starts.

Can I change packages later?

Yes. If your business adds accounts or needs more reporting, we can move you to a different package. Ask when your needs change.

What if my books need a cleanup first?

Then the cleanup is quoted as a separate one-time project. Once the books are current, your monthly package starts from accurate numbers.
