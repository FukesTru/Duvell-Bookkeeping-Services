A remote month, step by step

What does a fully remote month look like?

Here is how one month of bookkeeping runs for a Brooklyn business, from the first transaction to the monthly report.

  1. Transactions come in

    Bank and card feeds bring every transaction into your QuickBooks Online file.

  2. Each one is categorized

    Every transaction goes to the right account, and unclear ones go on a short email list for you.

  3. Bills and invoices are matched

    Payments are matched to the bills and invoices they belong to.

  4. Accounts are reconciled

    Each bank and credit card account is matched to its statement.

  5. The owner reviews the month

    Antonio checks the month for anything unusual before reports go out.

  6. You get your reports

    A profit and loss statement and balance sheet, explained in plain English. Growth and Premium add a monthly review call.

Questions

What do Brooklyn owners ask?

Do you ever meet Brooklyn clients in person?

Brooklyn clients work with us remotely, by phone, email and video. If you would like to meet in person, ask when you get in touch and we can talk about meeting near Jamaica, Queens.

Can you fix a QuickBooks file someone else set up?

Yes. A cleanup fixes duplicate entries, wrong categories and unreconciled accounts, then brings the file back to a starting point you can trust.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Duvell Bookkeeping Services does not use long-term contracts for monthly bookkeeping. The proposal you receive explains how the service runs and how to end it.

Can you send invoices to my customers?

Customer invoicing is part of our receivables work. In the consultation we agree on exactly which invoicing tasks you want us to handle.
