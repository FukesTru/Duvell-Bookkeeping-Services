Meet the owner of Duvell Bookkeeping Services

One owner. Your books.

Call (800) 380-1976Contact us

Duvell Bookkeeping Services is a solo bookkeeping practice in Jamaica, Queens, New York, owned and run by Antonio D. Sullivan. Antonio has worked in accounting since 2013 and holds a Master's degree in Accounting. Every client works directly with Antonio, from the first call to the monthly reports.

Background

What background does Antonio bring to your books?

Antonio began in entry-level accounting positions in 2013 and worked up to corporate accounting responsibilities before founding Duvell Bookkeeping Services.

Along the way that meant hands-on work with bank reconciliations, accounts payable, accounts receivable, payroll, invoicing, financial reporting and month-end close. Those are the same jobs a small business needs done well every month.

The lesson from those years was simple. A business runs better on accurate records, organized systems and someone you can trust to keep them that way.

Experience

Which industries has Antonio worked in?

Antonio has supported organizations in 4 settings. Each one has its own habits around billing, payroll and reporting, and that experience carries over to small businesses in other fields.

Hospitality

Businesses with daily sales, tips and many vendor bills, where cash moves quickly.

Nonprofit organizations

Organizations that report to boards and funders and need every dollar accounted for.

Education

Schools and education settings with payroll, tuition and program spending to track.

Administrative operations

Office and administrative teams where invoicing, payables and filing keep things running.

How the firm works

Why work with a solo practice?

When you call, email or text, you reach the owner. The person who reconciled your accounts is the person who answers your question about them.

  • Your books are reviewed personally every month, with no layered teams in between.
  • Reports come with a plain-English explanation of what the numbers say.
  • Cloud accounting and automation handle the repetitive steps, and a person checks the results.
  • The goal is a long working relationship built on accuracy, integrity and quick replies.
