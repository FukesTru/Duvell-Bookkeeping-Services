Each month

What happens in a month of bookkeeping?

The same 5 steps run every month, so your books never drift far from reality.

Categorizing means assigning each transaction to the right account in your chart of accounts, such as rent, supplies or sales. The chart of accounts is simply the list of those categories.

  1. Transactions come in

    Bank and card feeds bring every transaction into QuickBooks Online.

  2. Each one is categorized

    Every transaction is assigned to the right account, and unclear ones go on a short list for you.

  3. Accounts are reconciled

    Each bank and credit card account is matched to its statement.

  4. The books are reviewed

    The owner checks the month for anything unusual before reports go out.

  5. You get your reports

    A profit and loss statement and balance sheet, explained in plain English.

Your part

What do we need from you each month?

Very little. Most of the work happens without you.

  • Access to your QuickBooks Online file, with bank and card feeds connected.
  • Answers to a short list of transactions we could not identify.
  • Receipts for larger purchases, so they are recorded correctly.
  • A quick note when something changes, such as a new bank account or a new loan.

Who it suits

Which businesses is monthly bookkeeping right for?

Monthly bookkeeping comes in 3 packages, sized by how many accounts you have and how much reporting you want.

Solo operators and new businesses

Up to 2 bank or credit card accounts, monthly categorizing and reconciliation, and a monthly profit and loss statement and balance sheet.

Growing businesses

Up to 5 accounts, plus bills and invoices tracked, class or job tracking when needed and a monthly review call.

Established businesses

Accounts as scoped, a custom reporting package, a month-end close, cash flow tracking and direct owner access.

Questions

What do people ask about monthly bookkeeping?

How often will I get reports?

Every month. Once the month is reconciled you receive a profit and loss statement and a balance sheet, with a short note on anything worth your attention.

Do I need QuickBooks Online?

QuickBooks Online is the main platform we support. If you use other software, tell us when you reach out and we will talk through your options in the consultation.

Who owns my books?

You do. Your QuickBooks Online file belongs to your business. We work inside it, and you can log in at any time.

What if my books are already a mess?

Then a cleanup usually comes first, so monthly bookkeeping starts from accurate numbers. Read how a bookkeeping cleanup works.

Is monthly bookkeeping the same as doing my taxes?

No. Monthly bookkeeping keeps your records accurate all year. Your tax preparer then uses those records for your return. This website does not give tax advice.
