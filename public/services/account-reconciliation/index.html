Bank and credit card reconciliation

Every account, matched.

Account reconciliation means checking that every transaction in your books matches your bank and credit card statements. Duvell Bookkeeping Services, based in Jamaica, Queens, New York, reconciles small business accounts every month, remotely, so errors, missing entries and duplicates are caught before they reach your reports.

Why it matters

Why does reconciliation matter so much?

Every report, tax return and loan application starts from your account balances. If those are wrong, everything built on them is wrong too.

Reconciliation is the check that proves the books match the real world. It is the single step that makes the rest of your bookkeeping trustworthy.

Accurate reportsYour profit and loss statement and balance sheet reflect what really happened.

Ready for tax timeYour tax preparer starts from balances that match the statements.

Ready for a lenderBanks ask for financials, and reconciled books hold up to questions.

Problems found earlyUnusual charges show up within a month, not a year later.

What it finds

What problems does reconciliation catch?

  • Duplicate transactionsA payment entered by hand and also pulled in by the bank feed.
  • Missing transactionsBank fees, interest or transfers that never made it into the books.
  • Wrong amountsA typo that turned 152 dollars into 125 dollars.
  • Uncleared checksChecks written months ago that were never cashed.
  • Charges you do not recognizeCard charges worth a closer look.
  • Transfers in the wrong placeMoney moved between accounts and recorded as income or expense.

The process

How is each account reconciled?

  1. Get the statement

    We use the official bank or card statement for the month.

  2. Match every line

    Each transaction in the books is matched to the statement.

  3. Fix the gaps

    Missing, duplicate and wrong entries are corrected.

  4. Confirm the balance

    The ending balance in the books equals the statement.

How much does this cost?

Reconciliation is included in every monthly bookkeeping package. The number of accounts you have is one of the main things that sets your monthly price.

Questions

What do people ask about reconciliation?

How often should accounts be reconciled?

Every month. Monthly reconciliation catches problems while the details are still fresh and easy to fix.

Which accounts do you reconcile?

Your business bank accounts and credit cards, plus any other balance sheet accounts we agree on in your proposal.

What if an account has never been reconciled?

Then it needs a catch-up first. We work back through the statements until the balance in your books matches the bank.

Is reconciliation the same as checking my bank balance?

No. Reconciliation compares every single transaction, so an error in one entry cannot hide behind a balance that looks about right.
