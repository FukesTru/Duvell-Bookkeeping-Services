Questions

How do the services fit together?

Every service feeds the same set of books. Reconciled accounts make the reports reliable, and tracked bills and invoices make the cash flow picture complete.

See how pricing works

Which service should I start with?

Most clients start with monthly bookkeeping. If your books are behind or messy, a cleanup comes first so the monthly work begins from accurate numbers.

Can I pick only one service?

Yes. Some clients only need payroll entries recorded or receivables tracked. The consultation is where we work out what you actually need.

Do you work in software other than QuickBooks Online?

QuickBooks Online is the main platform we support. If you use something else, tell us when you reach out and we will talk through your options.

Are the services available outside New York?

Yes. Duvell Bookkeeping Services works remotely, so every service is available to small businesses anywhere in the US.
