Privacy

Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Duvell Bookkeeping Services collects through this website, how it is used and the choices you have. Duvell Bookkeeping Services is a business name of Duvell Industries LLC, based in Jamaica, New York. Last updated November 13, 2026.

Who we are

Duvell Bookkeeping Services is a bookkeeping practice owned by Duvell Industries LLC at 147-35 95th Ave, Jamaica, NY 11435. You can reach us by phone at (800) 380-1976 or by email at info@duvellbookkeeping.com.

What information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us and some basic technical information.

  • Details you enter in the website form, such as your name, phone number, email address and message.
  • Messages you send through the chat window on this website.
  • Details you share by phone or email.
  • Basic technical information that websites receive automatically, such as your browser type, the pages you visit and the date and time of your visit.

Please do not send bank account numbers, Social Security numbers, tax ID numbers or passwords through the website form or chat.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a consultation.
  • To prepare a proposal and provide the services you ask for.
  • To keep records of our conversations with you.
  • To keep this website working and secure.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with other businesses for their own marketing.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may contact you by phone call or text message about your enquiry. Message frequency depends on your enquiry, and message and data rates may apply. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. You can also ask us to stop by calling (800) 380-1976 or emailing info@duvellbookkeeping.com.

Service providers

The website form and chat window are provided by LeadConnector, a customer relationship platform. Information you send through them is stored by that provider on our behalf so we can respond to you. These tools may set cookies or similar technologies to work properly and to remember your preferences. Our website is hosted by a web hosting provider that processes basic technical information to deliver the pages to you.

Cookies

This website itself does not use advertising cookies. The form and chat tools described above may use cookies, and your browser settings let you block or delete them. Some features may not work if you block them.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information for as long as we need it to respond to you and to keep reasonable business records. If you become a client, records are kept as your engagement and the law require.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@duvellbookkeeping.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is meant for business owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
