Read all questions and answers

How much does bookkeeping cost at Duvell Bookkeeping Services?

Every client is priced after a free 30-minute consultation. The price depends on how many bank and credit card accounts you have, how many transactions you run each month, the state of your books today and the level of service you want. You get a written proposal with a flat price before any work starts.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Duvell Bookkeeping Services does not use long-term contracts for monthly bookkeeping. The proposal you receive explains how the service runs and how to end it.

Which accounting software do you work in?

QuickBooks Online is the main platform we support. If your books live in different software, mention it when you get in touch and we will talk through the options in your consultation.

Can you help if I am months or years behind?

Yes. Catch-up work and multi-year cleanups are some of the most common projects we take on. We rebuild and reconcile the missing months, then you can move onto monthly bookkeeping if you want to stay current.

Will my books be ready for my tax preparer?

That is the aim of monthly bookkeeping. Reconciled accounts and clean reports give your tax preparer an accurate starting point. This website does not give tax advice, so talk to your tax preparer about your own return.

How do we stay in touch?

Email is the usual way, with phone or video calls when something needs a conversation. Because Duvell Bookkeeping Services is a solo practice, you reach the owner directly every time.
