Questions

What do owners ask before they hire us?

Short answers to the questions we hear most.

Read all questions and answers

Do I need bookkeeping if I'm a solo business owner?

Yes. Clean books help you understand profitability, manage cash flow, and avoid scrambling at tax time. Even simple businesses benefit from organized records.

I'm way behind. Is it too late?

Not at all. Catch-up and multi-year cleanups are some of the most common projects I handle. We’ll get you current and back on solid ground.

Do you only work with QuickBooks?

QuickBooks Online is the primary platform supported. If you’re on something else, let’s talk. We can usually find a workable path forward.

How do we communicate?

Email is the default, with quick calls or video chats as needed. Because Duvell is a solo practice, you reach the owner directly, not a support queue.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Duvell Bookkeeping Services does not use long-term contracts for monthly bookkeeping. The proposal you receive explains how the service runs and how to end it.
